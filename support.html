Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Open TheorySprout, go to the subscription or paywall screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, your Pro access will be restored.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, select TheorySprout, and choose to change or cancel your plan. You can also cancel any time before a free trial ends to avoid being charged.
What does the Pro subscription include?
Pro unlocks unlimited practice across all grades and topics. A monthly and a yearly plan are available, each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
Is my data private?
Yes. TheorySprout does not collect any personal information. Your practice records and settings stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
I think a question or answer is wrong.
Please email us the grade, topic, and what you saw, and we'll review and correct it. Reports like this genuinely help improve the app.
